PART 2: Explaining an idea (steady, clear)

Let me explain with a simple example. Suppose a class reports that the average score is sixty. If everyone scored close to sixty, that number describes the class well. But if half the students scored thirty and half scored ninety, the average is still sixty, and not one student is anywhere near it. That is why we always draw a histogram next to the average. The plot tells us whether the average deserves our trust.
