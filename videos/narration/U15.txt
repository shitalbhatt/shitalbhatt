L:7 | Data leakage | Data leakage means the model sees information it will not have when used for real. Target leakage: a feature created after the outcome, like payment confirmed to predict buying. Train and test contamination: the same row in both parts, like our one hundred twenty five duplicates. Preprocessing leakage: a scaler, imputer or feature selection fitted on all rows before the split. And sampling leakage: over-sampling before the split, so copies of a row land in train and test. The result is great scores in the notebook and poor results in real life.
L:8 | Leakage on real data | Here is leakage on real data: the same model in two orders. Over-sample then split gives a recall of point nine nine two, the leaky number. Ninety eight point four percent of the leaky test buyers had an exact copy in the training set, so the model was tested on rows it had already memorised. The honest point six six five is lower, and it is the number you can trust.
L:9 | Preventing data leakage | How to prevent leakage. Remove duplicates first. Split first, before any fitting or resampling. Fit every step on training rows only. Resample, over or under, the training rows only. Ask of every feature: is it known at prediction time? And use the test set once. A pipeline, like make pipeline with a standard scaler and logistic regression, re-fits the scaler inside each cross-validation fold, so there is no peeking.
