# Channel launch guide

## Channel setup (week 0)
- **Name:** pick something searchable, for example "Data Analysis Lab" or "EDA with Python".
- **About text:** "Free lectures and Python labs for exploratory data analysis, data cleaning and feature engineering, for B.Tech and data science students. New videos every Monday, Wednesday and Friday."
- **Banner:** text "Learn EDA and Data Cleaning in Python" with the module colours used on the thumbnails.
- **Channel verification:** verify the channel at youtube.com/verify so custom thumbnails and long uploads work.
- **Playlists (create 4):**
  1. Module 1 - Statistical and Data Foundations for EDA (8 videos)
  2. Module 2 - Univariate, Bivariate, Multivariate EDA and Visualization (9 videos)
  3. Module 3 - Data Quality and Anomaly Management (8 videos)
  4. Module 4 - Feature Engineering, Transformation and Pipelines (12 videos)
- **Pinned comment (each video):** "Next lesson: <title>. Notebook and dataset are linked in the description. What topic should I cover next?"

## Weekly routine
1. **Mon / Wed / Fri 18:30 IST:** a long video goes live (scheduled by the upload script).
2. **Next day 12:00 IST:** its Short goes live.
3. **Within 24 hours:** reply to every comment, then share the video once in your class WhatsApp group, LinkedIn and one relevant community.
4. **End of each week:** open YouTube Studio → Analytics. Note which thumbnails and titles get the highest click-through rate, and reuse their style.

## Things that help views
- Put the notebook and dataset on GitHub and link it in every description.
- Add end screens that point to the next video in the playlist.
- Reuse the same thumbnail style (already done: one colour per module).
- Do not delete low-performing videos; edit the title and thumbnail after two weeks instead.
- Ask viewers one question per video so comments grow.

## Honest expectations
Educational channels usually need 6 to 18 months to grow. Views come from search (titles with the phrases people type) and from the recommended list, and both build up slowly. Monetization needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 public watch hours (or 10 million Shorts views) in the YouTube Partner Program.
