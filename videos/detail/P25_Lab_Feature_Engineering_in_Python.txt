Let me explain why we choose ROC AUC and a stratified split. With only fifteen percent buyers, a model that always predicts no would be eighty-five percent accurate and useless. ROC AUC measures how well the model ranks buyers above non-buyers, whatever threshold you use. Stratifying the split keeps fifteen percent buyers in both the train and the test parts, so the score is stable.
